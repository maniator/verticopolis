- (note) Origin: owner report (Pixel 8a, fully zoomed out, top speed, stutter at morning/evening rushes) + profiled save sixseven_8.vctower (4-star, 5,085 pop, 20,155 units, 24 shafts, floors -8..91); diagnosis session 2026-07-14.
- (note) Diagnosis: sim exonerated (tick ~7.5ms/sim-min flat all day; 55-112ms total tick time per 30s gameplay window); frames ~1s at MIN_ZOOM under 4x throttle at EVERY hour from ~2,500 retained actors; 1,635 room actors each own a private ex.Canvas texture vs 8-16 mobile GPU texture slots per batch (ImageRenderer flushes per slot exhaustion, Excalibur 0.32); hourly onHour scans (50-110ms) share a frame with the full render reconcile.
- (decision) Party-ratified (Cloud/Link/Samus, Grumbal walk-on): three stories in order S1 zoom cull, S2 shared bakes, S3 deferred hour reconcile; engine onHour amortization REJECTED as rider (determinism/golden-master load-bearing), backlogged pending checkpoint-inputs design.
- (capability) CAP-1 (S1): below a named sub-legible zoom threshold, crowd/vehicle actors (people, walkers, elevator cars, train, trucks, garage cars) are culled AND their per-frame position/graphic loops in updateMotion/reconcileCrowd are skipped; hysteresis (re-show above a higher zoom than hide) prevents strobing at the boundary. Success: rush probe on the owner save at MIN_ZOOM shows the crowd/vehicle share of frame time eliminated; actors reappear correctly when zooming in; no strobe across the threshold.
- (capability) CAP-2 (S2): room actors share IMMUTABLE baked canvases keyed by the existing draw signature (refcounted cache, evicted at zero refs); a unit look change swaps its bake reference and never mutates a shared canvas; animated rooms keep private canvases. Success: e2e visual baselines byte-identical; distinct room textures collapse from ~1,635 (per unit) to the live signature population (dozens); rush-probe frame times at MIN_ZOOM drop materially on the owner save.
- (capability) CAP-3 (S3): the hour-boundary full render reconcile (syncScene on d.hour change) defers one frame so it never shares a frame with the engine onHour scans. Success: the on-the-hour longest-frame in the rush probe drops by roughly the reconcile share; no visual state is ever skipped (the reconcile still runs, one frame later).
- (constraint) Render/presentation-only: zero engine-number changes, engine stays DOM-free, golden master byte-stable, canon parity untouched (Samus condition: a camera-and-paint patch).
- (constraint) Cull threshold is a named constant with the sub-legible rationale (figure under ~3 screen px) written beside it; walker BUILD stays structural-revision-gated (never rebuilt on zoom changes) per Cloud.
- (constraint) Shared bakes are immutable by contract (Grumbal trap: mutating a shared canvas repaints every sharing unit); signature under-keying is guarded by the pinned visual baselines failing the PR.
- (constraint) Per story: all four quality gates + the CI perf gate; /gds-code-review with findings fixed and defers backlogged; rush-probe before/after on sixseven_8.vctower in the PR body.
