# Transparency note (player-facing copy)

Spec-authored companion to `SPEC.md`. This is the ready-to-drop copy for the "what we measure and why" line that ships with the analytics change (Help or Settings, wherever the privacy line lives). Keep it plain and human, American English, no em-dashes.

## Short line (Settings footer or Help privacy row)

> **What we measure.** The game keeps a few anonymous counts to see how new players get started and how far returning players get. We never store who you are, use no cookies, and nothing about you leaves your device except those anonymous counts. There is nothing to accept or turn off.

## Longer paragraph (Help "Privacy" subsection, if one exists)

> Verticopolis keeps a small, anonymous read on how the game is going: whether new players place their first facility, how far towers climb the star ladder, which tools get used, and whether players who come back get further than first-timers. The "returning" and "how far along" signals are worked out on your own device and sent only as coarse brackets (for example "week 1" or "reached 3 stars"), never as anything that could point back to you. No cookies, no account, no tracking across sites, and no consent banner, because there is nothing here to consent to. The counts help decide what to improve; they are never sold or shared.

## Copy rules

- No em-dashes; commas, colons, parentheses, or separate sentences instead.
- No "X, not Y" emphatic-restatement pattern; no AI-marketing vocabulary.
- The claim must stay true to `SPEC.md`: only anonymous, low-cardinality, on-device-derived counts leave the device, and no persistent identifier is transmitted. If the SPEC's data posture changes, this copy changes with it.
