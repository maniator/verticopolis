Tenant Churn

Four voices, one mechanic, and the question of how much a tower should be allowed to hurt.

“Hurt them — but inform prior.” The eviction must always feel earned.

The mechanic, in one breath

A dissatisfied office or condo goes on notice for two in-game days instead of vanishing. Fix the cause and let satisfaction climb back over the line — they stay. Let the clock run out — they leave, and the card told you exactly why and exactly when.

rescind at 0.25 → rescind at 0.40 “stabilized ≠ fixed”

What the room decided

Shipped · PR #109

1 · Hurt them, but inform prior

  • Rescind bar 0.25 → 0.40 — “stabilized ≠ fixed”; a real fix still recovers in ~8 served hours.
  • Notice window stays 2 days — shortening it reopens per-rush-hour eviction spam (Cloud’s veto).
  • Inform prior: inspector shows an honest “Leaves in under N hours” countdown + “get to 40% to keep them (now X%).”
Shipped · PR #109

  • Balance change guarded by the well-zoned endgame + TOWER parity runs — a healthy tower still doesn’t bleed.
