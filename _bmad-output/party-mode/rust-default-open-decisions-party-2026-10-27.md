# Rust-default 3.0: the open decisions, second party sitting

> **Snapshot of Tuesday, Oct 27, 2026 (evening UTC), against the plan on PR #1348 at head b8aef0e. It does not refresh.** This is the second sitting of the Cross-Cutting Plan Review room on the Rust-default replan; the first sitting's transcript is `_bmad-output/party-mode/rust-default-replan-party-2026-10-27.md`. The bounds below are exact one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson computations the facilitator ran outside the repository and agree with the project's `upperBound` in `scripts/engine-reliability.mjs`; the repository does not yet pin the 1,000-session rows with 2 to 5 failures or the 50-session row, so they are planning figures and none of them is published as a result. The day counts are projections from the governed `session_lengths` traffic figure of about 14.2 engaged eligible sessions a day at 100%. Tess's detectable-gap figures are her own arithmetic, labeled as hers in her speech. The live gate state is #1104. Nothing here activates a stage, flips a default, deploys, publishes 3.0.0, adds a production analytics field or grants an exception; those stay with Naftali.

## What was asked

After the plan on PR #1348 recorded decisions 1 to 4, it listed what was still open, and named one recommendation (the .TDT half of #1066) as the owner's to confirm because it reverses his OD-8 and D5. Naftali answered "Take to the party". The room took the open list as five decisions:

- **A.** The .TDT reversal of OD-8 and D5.
- **B.** What counts, and whether the 300 carry into the 1,000 (B1), whether a Settings move to TypeScript is a failure (B2), and whether opted-out sessions are in the count (B3).
- **C.** The pass rule for the 1,000 (C1), and the comparison once the TypeScript arm ends at 100% (C2).
- **D.** Coverage cells: a browser or device-class field (D1) and a distinct-player floor (D2).
- **E.** Ladder definitions: when `?engine=ts` leaves (E1), "fallback unused" (E2), "a release" (E3), and the date of slice 11 inside 3.x (E4).

## Checked by the facilitator between the rounds

- `docs/analytics-data-map.md` (lines 23 to 26) says `distinct_id` is a random id kept in `sessionStorage`, that it dies with the tab, and that nothing links one visit to the next. There is no install-level or player-level identifier. Anika's "largest single-install share" and Grumbal's "per install" cannot be computed from governed data. What exists is `returning`, the `tenure` and `recency` buckets, `display` and `platform`. There is no browser-family field.
- `platform` is the closed set `web` or `twa` (`src/analyticsIngest.ts`, `INGEST_PLATFORM_LABELS`), so it does not split phones from desktops. Tess's condition for dropping the browser field ("if the existing platform field already splits phones") is therefore not met.
- Exact bounds: 1,000 sessions with 4 failures is 0.913%; with 3, 0.774%; with 5, 1.048%. A fixed read at exactly 1,000 is clean. An extension after a failure is not (Codex P2).
