# Welcome window text: party findings, 2026-11-14

Room: Player Panel (bmad-party-mode, subagent mode: each voice answered on
its own). Question from Naftali: "What happened to the previous text? Why
change it?" The first draft said "Welcome to Verticopolis™ 4.0!" and "This
release brings a new menu bar. Thanks for building with us." The owner then
asked for no version and for first-time links, and the rewrite dropped the
menu bar line and the thanks along with the version.

## The round

🍎 **Marco:** Blend them, but don't paste the first draft back in. The
greeting line just repeats the title bar. One menu bar sentence gives the
returning players their nod without confusing anyone new.

🏢 **Kenji:** Put the greeting back in the body; the 1994 game told you what
you were about to do. Tuck the menu bar into the returning players' line:
new players never had the old sidebar.

♿ **Wren:** No second greeting: my screen reader reads the dialog title
first, so a bold repeat means I hear it twice. But a returning player needs
to hear the menu bar is new, because File, Edit, Tower, Help appearing is the
one thing that confuses a keyboard user. Keep the thanks.

🤖 **Lucia:** Keep it tight; every line pushes OK down my phone. The links
are the useful part for new players, the menu bar is the reason the window
exists, and the thanks is the only warm line. Put it all in the returning
players' line rather than add a line.

🌐 **Priya:** Say the menu bar in plain words; "See what's new" behind a link
is easy to miss. The thanks works for newcomers too.

## Verdict

- The menu bar line comes back (5 of 5), in the returning players' line
  (Kenji, Wren, Lucia; Marco and Priya wanted it in the opening paragraph).
- "Thanks for building with us" comes back (Priya, Wren, Lucia), at the end
  of the returning players' line, where it reads right to the people it
  thanks (Kenji's and Marco's worry about newcomers).
- No second greeting in the body: the title bar already says "Welcome to
  Verticopolis™" (Wren, Marco; Kenji outvoted).
- No added line, so OK stays on a phone screen (Lucia).
- Still no version anywhere, and the three links stay.
