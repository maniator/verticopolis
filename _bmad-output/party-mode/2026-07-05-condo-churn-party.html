An office is a lease you manage.

The reframe that broke the deadlock — the bug was never the money, it was running both through the same vacate() verb.

The fork they left open — on purpose

What should a condo’s satisfaction actually be, once it stops being a hair-trigger for walkouts?

Satisfaction = value

Drives sale price, resale, maybe reputation. The noisy neighbor costs you money at sale, not a walkout. Faithful — but risks going inert once the unit’s sold and sitting.

Satisfaction = a patient flight risk

Sticky against spikes, but sustained rock-bottom neglect eventually loses the owner. A much longer fuse than the office’s two-day trigger. Keeps the heatmap honest.

The room’s lean: patient flight risk — the smallest change that keeps satisfaction load-bearing. Cloud’s “should star rating be able to fall?” was left on the whiteboard, deliberately un-opened.
