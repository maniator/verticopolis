## Findings

- **F1 (new; HP-8, HP-9, HP-10; section 7 always-`patch` class: a change that bypasses a cache).** `tsStatsReport` (`src/simport/tsPanels.ts` 49) calls `forgetRoomCounts(sim.tower)` and then `sim.stats()`, so every stats open recounts every room where the ledger already answers. Entry point and tier: `showStats` (`src/game/appModals.ts`) → `port.query({ op: "statsReport" })` → `tsStatsReport`, per command (each open, and the redraw after `confirmExterminate`), on the TypeScript engine and on both WASM hosts' read model (the app's port is `createTsSim` over `app.sim`). Sizes: R = 2,900 large, 1,699 owner's save. Estimate: one pass over the rooms with a `Share` allocation and a `Map` insert per room, about 11,600 visits; measured 0.495 ms per open on large (0.216 medium, 0.369 owner's save) against 0.003 to 0.005 ms for the ledger read, about 8% of a 5.75 ms open on large. TypeScript equivalent: on `main` the report's `stats()` walked every unit and the population again (1.89 ms), so the open is cheaper than `main`; the finding is against the cache this PR adds. HP-10: the drop also re-baselines the HUD's ledger on every open, so a missed mark heals itself whenever a player opens the dialog, the drift that round 3 dismissed a periodic recount for (B1) because "a recount would hide drift" from the guard and the tests. Remedy (HP-9 item 4, read the cache): read the ledger in the report like the HUD, and move the drop to the writers it exists for, the tooling scenes that stage counted fields without marks (`scripts/screenshot-tower-builders.ts` and the scene that stages vacancies), either by calling `forgetRoomCounts` after staging through the tooling handle or by writing through `setRoomState`/`markRoom`; the screenshot drift check catches a scene that misses it. Risk: the report then shows the HUD's counts, so any production writer without a mark shows in both (the guard and the tests are the net either way). Not in any open issue. A deferral needs the owner's acceptance in his own words (section 7).
