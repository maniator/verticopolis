## Weighing the points the caller asked about

- **The marks on the engine step.** A mark is a `WeakMap.get` and a `Set.add`, 26 to 53 ns measured, made at a site that already visits the room, and a no-op (one `WeakMap` miss) before the tower's first read. Marks match the rooms really changed (no redundant marks over three game days): mean about 4 per 2-minute step on large, maximum 1,439 in the hourly pass that serves notices, so at most about 76 microseconds in one step. No HP-2 finding. Per person, `beginDwell` and `finish` add one mark per venue arrival and departure, at most the drawn crowd (P at most 140 plus staff) per chunk.
- **The marks on the frame apply.** The per-record check is the measurable cost of this PR: F1.
- **The recount after each host merge, and how often merges happen.** F2: 0.25 to 0.64 ms once per merge; at speed 3 that is every HUD read at night and about one in three over a day; small against the merge itself (135 to 313 ms TypeScript side).
- **The worst read after a step with many marks.** Bounded by R (the `Set` dedupes, and only rooms carry shares); measured 0.35 ms (682 rooms, hotel-heavy) to 1.02 ms (1,436 rooms, large, single read), once per hourly pass with mass changes. Inside F2.
- **Memory.** The ledger holds the counts, one share object per room in a `Map` and the `Set` of marks: 101 KB on medium, 131 KB on the owner's save and 252 KB on large (`hp1.out`, heap with and without, after `gc`). One ledger per `Tower`, keyed in a `WeakMap`, so a tower no longer used (an adopted-away save, a fault restore) takes its ledger with it; undo keeps strings and no `Tower`. The WASM mirror keeps its `Tower` across merges, and each merge deletes and the next read recreates its one entry, so nothing piles up. Marks on units since removed: a removal bumps the revision, the next read sees a new rooms list and replaces the ledger, so a removed unit stays referenced (by `shares` and possibly `changed`) only until that read. The `Set` grows only between reads and only with distinct units; it would keep growing only on a tower that was read once and then kept simulating with build and demolish churn and no further read, which no production path does (the HUD reads about 6 times a second whenever the frame loop steps the sim; a blocking modal or a hidden tab stops both). No leak found.
- **Anything rebuilding the ledger per frame.** No. On the TypeScript engine a rebuild needs a new rooms list (a place, a remove, a transport edit or a load: commands only; no step code bumps the revision or replaces the unit array) or a drop (the hosts' merge only). On the WASM hosts it rebuilds once per merge, that is per engine hour and per command; at speed 3 that approaches once per HUD read, never once per frame, since only the HUD reads it. A drag that places tiles on each pointer move rebuilds once per HUD read during the drag, a per-command cost the rooms list already pays.
