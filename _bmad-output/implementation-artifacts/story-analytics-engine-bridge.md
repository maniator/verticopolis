# Story: gameplay analytics from the engine's events, phase 2 (the bridge)

## Why

Phase 1 (#903) gave both engines a drain buffer of gameplay events, a catalog
as the contract, and an events hash in the conformance lock. Nothing read the
stream. This story is migration step 2: `src/analyticsEngineBridge.ts` drains
the live tower and feeds the platform's folds (`GameplaySession` and the action
trackers), and the shell's own detection sites retire. The wire names do not
change, so dashboards keep working; where the engine's truth differs from what
the shell guessed, the value changes and is documented.

## Plan (party, 2026-10-11)

A fan-out party (Winston, Grumbal, Boundary, Mary, Amelia, then John, Cloud
and Grumbal in cross-talk) settled the scope:

- Ship the bridge with the step-2 gates from the #873 backlog row, and retire
  every site whose engine source exists today: `new_game_started`, the build
  folds, `star_reached`, `session_emergencies`, `emergency_choice`,
  `price_tune` and `capacity_tune`. John cut Winston's proposed hold on the two
  tuning latches: their emission lives in the sim and does not wait on #928.
- `demolish` stays on the shell until #928 moves the bulldozer and the
  editor's Sell onto an engine removal command.
- Keep the shift and document it: `emergency_choice.decision` is what took
  effect (Cloud), `first_build.tool` is the kind placed, `price_tune` and
  `capacity_tune` latch only on a real change, editor builds count, and
  `star_reached` sends one row per rung.
- Preserve exactly: the funnel order and per-tower `first_build`, the
  `session_builds` unit (Mary's per-drag fold was dropped: the shell already

- Drain once per frame at the top of `runFrame`, ahead of the modal freeze

  window change (dropped unsent with the closing window; round 1 review).
  No separate swap drain: `adoptSim` already hands a replaced tower's events

- Tests: a fail-closed table, the rate limit with a clock, a batch spanning two
  towers, the backdrop skip, a multi-rung jump, a drain that throws once

  as the same wire events.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC1 One drain.** The frame loop drains the live tower once per frame,
   before the blocking-modal return. The session folds the engine's last
   events before `end` reports, and drains and discards them (with the window
   they belong to) before a new measurement window opens.
2. **AC2 Fail closed.** Every drained event is checked against the catalog's
   rules (`checkEvent`). One the catalog does not describe is dropped and
   counted, the rest of the batch still folds, and nothing throws into the
   frame loop. A drain that throws is counted and folds nothing.
3. **AC3 Only the folds, under a rate limit.** The bridge sends nothing itself:
   the folds send today's wire events through `trackEvent`. Per-occurrence
   sends (`new_game_started`, `star_reached`, `emergency_choice`) pass a
   per-name rate limit; counting is never limited.
4. **AC4 Folds count actions.** Every `facility_placed` counts, including a
   build the player then undid; `session_builds` sums `count`.
5. **AC5 Two towers in one batch.** Only `tower_founded` re-opens the
   per-tower funnel; a load or an undo hands events on with no founding.
6. **AC6 The backdrop.** A buffer marked `discardOnHandOff` is drained and
   dropped, and the catalog's `tower_founded` states the backdrop rule.
7. **AC7 Frozen catalog.** The catalog is pinned by digest for the release
   that ships the bridge.
8. **AC8 Shell sites retired.** Outside the bridge and the tests, nothing in `src/` calls
   `noteNewGame`, `noteBuild`, `noteStar`, `trackEmergencyChoice` or
   `trackEconomyActionOnce` any more, and the emergency counter sampler is gone.
9. **AC9 Both engines.** The same play on the TypeScript engine and under the
   WASM host reaches the provider as the same wire events, in order.
10. **AC10 Documented.** The value changes are written where the wire
    vocabulary and the #611 event catalog are read.

## What landed

- `src/analyticsEngineBridge.ts`: `drainEngineEvents` (the frame loop's call),
  `forwardGameplayEvents`, `bindEngineBridge`, the drop
  counters (`engineBridgeStats`) and the rate limit (`BRIDGE_SEND_LIMIT`, 20
  per wire name per minute, reusing `RateLimiter`).
- `src/engine/gameplayEventRules.ts`, generated by `npm run gen:events`: the
  catalog's enums and payload fields without the prose, so the bridge can run
  `checkEvent` in the shipped game. `checkEvent` takes the narrower
  `GameplayEventRules`.
- `GameplaySession`: `notePlaying`, `noteEmergency` and `setEngineFlush`. The emergency sampler and its banking across
  towers are gone; the events sum across towers by construction.
- Retired call sites: `saveLoad.newGame`, `BuildActions.tryBuild`,
  `tryBuildTransport` and `paintBrush`, `KeyboardPlay`'s shaft commit, the star
  diff's `noteStar` (the promote jingle stays), the counter sampler, the
  emergency modal's `trackEmergencyChoice`, and the eight editor
  `trackEconomyActionOnce` calls.
- `appBoot` binds the bridge to `app.sim`.
- Catalog: the `tower_founded` backdrop rule and the digest pin.
- Docs: the wire vocabulary in `analyticsCore.ts` and the #611 event catalog.
