---
title: Stars and the TOWER
description: Every star rating in Verticopolis, the population each needs, the services each asks for, and how to earn the TOWER and win.
---

Your tower starts with one star. Stars are how the city rates it, and each one unlocks new rooms and bigger plans. Population earns a star, but past two stars population alone isn't enough: the tower also has to show it can look after the people in it.

## The ratings

| Rating | People | And the tower needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ★ | Any | Nothing: this is where you start |
| ★★ | 300 | Nothing more |
| ★★★ | 1,000 | Two Security offices |
| ★★★★ | 5,000 | A Medical Center, enough recycling for everyone, two hotel suites, and a VIP's good review |
| ★★★★★ | 10,000 | A Metro Station |
| TOWER | 15,000 | The Wedding Hall, and the VIP's approval at the inspection |

Each rating needs everything the ones below it asked for, too. Your rating never falls once earned.

### Who counts

Up to four stars, everyone counts: office workers, residents, hotel guests and the visitors in your shops. From four stars on, the city stops counting hotel guests, so for five stars and the TOWER only the people who work and live in the tower (and their visitors) count. A big hotel helps you reach four stars, but it won't carry you past.

## Two to five

**Two stars** unlocks the first services, Security and Housekeeping, along with single hotel rooms and the service elevator.

**Three stars** opens up most of the game: restaurants, shops, theaters, escalators, express elevators, parking, the Medical Center and recycling. In Modern, all the new venues appear. You need **two** Security offices before the tower can reach it.

**Four stars** is the hard one. It asks for:

- a **Medical Center**,
- enough **Recycling Centers** for your whole population (each handles 2,500),
- at least **two hotel suites**, and
- a **favorable review from a VIP**. From three stars, a VIP may stay in one of your suites. They need a suite they like (rated 70% or better) and a [parking space](/help/rooms/services/#parking) for their car. If they're unhappy, or have nowhere to park, they try again a few days later.

**Five stars** needs a working **Metro Station**, which unlocks at four.

## The TOWER

At five stars you can build the [Wedding Hall](/help/rooms/wedding-hall/) on top of your tower. Three days after it opens, a VIP comes to inspect. If the tower has 15,000 people who work or live there, five stars, a Metro and the Wedding Hall, the VIP declares it a **TOWER**, and you win. If not, they come back five days later.

## Milestones

Along the way, the game marks milestones in the message log:

| Milestone | For |
| --- | --- |
| Getting Started | 500 people |
| Rising | 2,500 people |
| Metropolis | 7,500 people |
| Almost There | 12,000 people |
| Four Stars | Reaching four stars |
| Five Stars | Reaching five stars |
| Showtime | Opening a cinema |
| On the Map | Opening a Metro Station |
| Touch the Sky | Building up to floor 100 |
| Smooth Operator | 5,000 people, with every occupied floor served |
| No Vacancy | 2,000 people, with every office, condo and hotel room taken |
