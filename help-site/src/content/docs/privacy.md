---
title: Privacy
description: What Verticopolis counts, what it never collects, and where your saves live.
---

Verticopolis keeps a small, anonymous read on how the game is going: whether new players place their first facility, how far towers climb the star ladder, which tools get used, which menu commands, buttons and windows get used (opened, moved, resized, minimized, maximized or closed) and in what order, whether the Bulletin's older messages get read, how often the Sponsor link is tapped, how smoothly the game runs (its frame rate and how long the simulation takes per game hour, next to the tower's population and the game speed), and whether returning players get further than first-timers. Those signals are worked out on your own device and sent as coarse, anonymous counts to our own site, with no cookie and nothing that could point back to you across visits. The page also sends anonymous page-visit counts (the name of the site that linked you here but never the page you came from, whether you landed on the game's main page, and any campaign tag in the link you followed), along with page performance metrics. On this manual it counts which pages are read and where a visit started: the place in the game you opened it from (such as the Help menu or a dialog's link), or simply that it came from outside the game. That is a fixed label, never the address of a page.

To see whether the game engine runs reliably, the game reports which engine started, whether you started playing, and a short code if the engine stopped. None of it identifies you.

Crash reports carry the technical details of the error and the same kind of anonymous totals. Before one is sent, the game takes out any name of yours it can find in it, such as a tower's name, a file's name or a facility's name, and puts `<player text>` in its place. If an error quotes part of a file the game couldn't read, that part is taken out too.

The game sets one cookie, and only after you pick a look in Options > Appearance: `vc_scheme`, which holds the name of that look (`standard`, `skyline` or `high_contrast`) and nothing else. Your browser sends it to every verticopolis.com address, and two of them read it: this manual, at help.verticopolis.com, and the engine's developer docs, at docs.verticopolis.com, so both can wear the same look when you visit them on their own. It never goes to anyone else, it lasts up to 400 days, and it is not used to count or recognize you.

There are no accounts and no ads: the game never asks for your name or email, keeps no profile, and nothing recognizes you from one day to the next. There is no consent banner, because nothing that identifies you is kept. Saves live in your own storage and leave your device only when you export them. The counts help decide what to improve; they are never sold or shared.
