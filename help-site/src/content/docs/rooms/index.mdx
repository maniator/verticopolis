A tower is only as good as what you put in it. Offices pay the bills, homes and hotels fill the upper floors with people, shops and restaurants feed them, and a handful of services keep the whole place safe, clean and growing. This part of the manual takes each kind of room in turn: what it costs, what it earns, what its tenants ask of you, and how it changes the rooms around it.

Every number here comes straight from the game, so when the game changes, these pages change with it.

- **[Offices](/help/rooms/offices/)** pay rent every quarter and bring the weekday crowd.
- **[Homes](/help/rooms/homes/)**: condominiums you sell once, and in Modern, studios and apartments you rent by the month.
- **[Hotels](/help/rooms/hotels/)** earn every night, as long as Housekeeping keeps up.
- **[Food and shops](/help/rooms/food-and-shops/)** earn from the people already in your tower.
- **[Venues](/help/rooms/venues/)**: the cinema and party hall, and Modern's clubs, spas, pools and more.
- **[Services](/help/rooms/services/)**: Security, Medical, Recycling, parking and the Metro, the rooms your star rating depends on.
- **[The Wedding Hall](/help/rooms/wedding-hall/)**, the crown on top of a finished tower.

Lobbies, floors and the ways to get between them have their own part of the manual, [Building](/help/building/floors-and-lobbies/).

## Everything at a glance

Sizes are in tiles across and floors up. A room marked **Modern** can only be built in a Modern tower; see [Classic vs Modern](/help/classic-vs-modern/).
