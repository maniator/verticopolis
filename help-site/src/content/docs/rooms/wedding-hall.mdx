Every great building is finished with something on top. In Verticopolis it is the Wedding Hall, a five-story chapel and garden that crowns the tower, and it is how you win.

## Building it

The Wedding Hall unlocks at five stars. It is 28 tiles wide and five floors tall, and it costs $3,000,000, so save up.

<span class="vc-mode">Classic</span> As in 1994, the top floor of the lot belongs to the Wedding Hall alone. Nothing else may reach floor 100, and the hall stands on the floors below it with its garden open to the sky. Once built, it stays for good: there is no selling it.

<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> You may build anything you like on the top floors, and the Wedding Hall can be sold like other rooms.

## The VIP's inspection

Three days after the hall opens, a VIP comes to inspect the tower. To pass, your tower needs all of this on the day:

- the Wedding Hall, open
- five stars
- a working [Metro Station](/help/rooms/services/#metro)
- **15,000 people** who work or live in the tower (hotel guests don't count, even while they're out at dinner)

Pass, and your building is a **TOWER**, the highest rank there is. You win, and you can keep playing as long as you like.

If the VIP is unimpressed, they come back five days later. Use the time to grow.
