Nobody moves into a tower for its recycling center. But past a certain size, a tower without the right services stops growing: the city won't give it another star, tenants complain about the garbage, and sooner or later something catches fire. This page covers the rooms that keep a big tower running. Most of them cost you money every month instead of earning it, and they are worth every dollar.

| Service | Needed for | Also |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Security | 3★ (two offices) | Catches thieves, makes fires less likely, searches for bombs |
| Medical Center | 4★ | Makes fires less likely |
| Recycling Center | 4★ (enough for everyone) | Keeps garbage from wearing down ratings |
| Parking | 4★ (for the VIP's suite) | Offices, and in Modern homes, want spaces |
| Metro Station | 5★ | Brings shoppers in from the city |

The full list of what each star needs is on [Stars and the TOWER](/help/tower/stars/).

## Security

<RoomInfo kind="security" />

A Security office keeps six guards. The tower needs **two** Security offices before it can reach three stars.

Guards earn their keep in three ways. With any Security in the tower, a thief who slips in is caught; without it, the thief walks off with up to $25,000. Security makes a fire much less likely. And when a caller threatens to set off a bomb and you refuse to pay, the guards search the tower for it, floor by floor, walking the fire escapes on the outside of the tower (they never take the elevators). The bomb goes off at 1 PM, and the rest of the tower stands still until the guards find it. More offices mean more guards and a faster search. See [Events](/help/tower/events/).

## Medical Center

<RoomInfo kind="medical" />

Four stars needs a Medical Center. Having one also halves the chance of a fire.

## Recycling Center

<RoomInfo kind="recycling" />

A tower makes garbage. Each Recycling Center handles the waste of 2,500 people; through the day you can watch it fill, and a garbage truck empties it every morning at 5 AM.

From three stars, a tower with more people than its centers can handle starts to smell: the game warns you, and tenants' ratings suffer until you build more. Four stars needs enough recycling for everyone. Plenty of recycling also gives your shops a small boost.

<span class="vc-mode">Classic</span> Any shortfall at all drags ratings down.

<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> A small shortfall holds ratings at Good; only one more than a whole center's worth short drags them down.

## Parking

<div class="vc-sprite-row">
  <Sprite kind="parkingRamp" id="parkingRamp" small caption="Parking Ramp" />
  <Sprite kind="parking" small caption="Parking Space" />
</div>

Parking is underground. Build a **Parking Ramp** first, then lay **Parking Spaces** beside it. A space only works if it touches the ramp or another working space, so chain them off the ramp in a row; a space on its own sits empty.

Who needs a space:

- **Hotel suites**, one each, always. The VIP drives, and a VIP with nowhere to park circles the block and leaves without the review four stars needs.
- **Offices**, from three stars, out of whatever is left after the suites. <span class="vc-mode">Classic</span> Any spare space at all serves every office; none leaves them all unhappy. <span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> One space for every 24 workers, the lowest offices first.
- <span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> **Homes** need spaces too: one for every two condos, three apartments or six studios. Homes short of parking hold at Good and fill more slowly.

<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> A working Metro halves what offices and homes need. Suites still need their own.

## Metro Station

<RoomInfo kind="metro" />

The Metro is the biggest thing you will build underground: three floors tall, thirty tiles wide, and one per tower. It unlocks at four stars, and five stars needs it.

A working station brings people from all over the city into your shops and restaurants, a quarter more business than your own people bring, and it takes the sting out of rainy days. In Modern it also halves the parking your offices and homes need.

Riders get off on the station's middle floor, and that platform works like a lobby for shoppers. A shop, restaurant or theater within two floors of it does full business even deep underground. With the station on B8 to B10, the platform is B9, so B7 to B9 are good for shops, while B5 and B6 are too far from both the platform and the ground lobby and take half.

<span class="vc-mode">Classic</span> As in the 1994 game, the station only brings in shoppers once its platform is connected: build an elevator, stairs or an escalator down to it. The game warns you if the platform is cut off.

<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> Stairs and escalators built against the station's wall wind down to it: a spiral staircase, and an escalator that turns back halfway. See [Stairs and escalators](/help/building/stairs-and-escalators/#beside-a-venue).
