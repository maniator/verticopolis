---
title: Controls
description: Every way to play Verticopolis, with a mouse, by touch on a phone or tablet, or from the keyboard alone.
---

Pick a tool, then use it on the tower. The tools live in the **Tools** window, grouped by kind (Structure, Transport, Commercial and so on). On a phone each group has its own tab, and a tab with a dot has just gained new tools. **Inspect** opens a room's card, and **Bulldoze** sells a room back. See [The Tools window](/help/tower/map-and-windows/#the-tools-window) to fold it out of the way.

## Mouse

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Build | Click with a build tool. Drag to lay a run of floor, lobby or parking, or to size an elevator shaft. |
| Open a room's card | Choose **Inspect** and click the room, or right-click it with any tool |
| Peek at a room | Hover over it |
| Move around | Drag with **Inspect**. With a build tool, hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> and drag, or drag with the middle button. While you hold either key, a click or a tap builds nothing. |
| Zoom | Scroll the wheel |
| Move a window | Drag its blue title bar. Double-click the title bar to put it back. |
| Undo, redo | The **Undo** button, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> |

While you hold a room over the tower, a ghost shows where it would land: gold where it fits, red where it won't. <span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> Hovering a red spot tells you why before you click.

## Phone and tablet

Everything works by touch.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Place a room | Press the tower and drag. The ghost floats just above your finger so you can see it. Lift to place it. A quick tap drops one too. |
| Lay a run | Drag with **Floor**, **Lobby** or a **Parking** space |
| Size an elevator | Drag up or down. Stairs and escalators place with a single tap. |
| Move around | Drag with two fingers (or one finger with **Inspect** chosen) |
| Zoom | Pinch |
| Open a room's card | Tap it with **Inspect** |
| Peek at a room | Press and hold it with any tool. Lift to close. |
| See the whole tower | Tap the **Map** pill in the corner |

The **menu** button in the top bar opens the statistics and the game menu, and the **Undo** button steps a change back.

## Keyboard

You can play without a mouse at all. Tab to the build menu, press <kbd>Enter</kbd> on a tool, then:

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> | Move the build cursor (hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to move ten at a time) |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> | Build or inspect at the cursor. For an elevator or stairway, press once at one end and again at the other. |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd>, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> or <kbd>X</kbd> | Bulldoze at the cursor |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Cancel |
| <kbd>+</kbd> <kbd>−</kbd> | Zoom |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | Center the view |
| <kbd>0</kbd> to <kbd>3</kbd> | Pause, or run at speed 1, 2 or 3 |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> | Redo |

**On the Map** (Tab to it): the arrow keys move the view (hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> for a whole screen), <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> jump to the top and the bottom of the tower, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it. On the Map's **View** menu, <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> pick the previous or next view.
