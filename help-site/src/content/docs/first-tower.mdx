Every tower starts the same way: an empty lot, a street, and $2,000,000 of somebody else's money. This page walks you from there to your first rent and your first star. The game's **Getting Started** steps walk you through the first part too, and you can replay them any time from **About this game** in the Help menu.

## Found the tower

Choose **New Tower** on the opening screen. Give your tower a name, then pick its rules:

- **Classic** is the 1994 game, as faithfully as we can make it.
- **Modern** keeps all of that and adds what the original couldn't do: new rooms, rental homes, a gentler calendar and smarter elevators.

You choose once, for the life of the tower. Not sure? Modern is the friendlier start. See [Classic and Modern](/help/classic-vs-modern/) for the whole list.

## 1. Open your lobby

People arrive at street level, so every tower starts with a lobby on the ground floor. Pick **Lobby** and drag along the ground line, the row just above the dirt. Make it wide: everything you build above will sit on it.

<Sprite kind="lobby" caption="A lobby" />

## 2. Add a floor

Every room needs a floor under it. Pick **Floor** and drag across the row above your lobby. Floors are cheap, so lay a good stretch.

## 3. Lease an office

Offices pay the rent. Pick **Office** and drop one on your new floor. It sits empty for now, waiting for a company to move in.

<Sprite kind="office" caption="An office" />

## 4. Connect it

Nobody climbs the outside of a building. Your office needs a way down to the lobby. Pick **Stairway** and click on the lobby, which links it to the floor above. Or pick **Standard Elevator** and drag from the lobby up to your office's floor.

<Sprite kind="elevatorStandard" span={3} caption="A standard elevator" />

## 5. Press Play and wait

Press **Play** (or <kbd>1</kbd>). Within a day or two a company moves in, and its rent lands with the next bill. Your tower is open for business.

## Your first star

Your tower has one star. At **300 people** it earns a second, and that's when things get interesting: hotels, Security and Housekeeping unlock.

To get there:

- **Fill a few floors with offices.** Each holds six workers. Stack them up, one floor at a time, and run the elevator up with them.
- **Sell some condos.** A condo is a home that sells once for a lump sum, and brings a family. It's the quickest money early on. See [Homes](/help/rooms/homes/#condo).
- **Feed them.** Build a [Fast Food](/help/rooms/food-and-shops/#fastFood) place on a low floor. Office workers need somewhere to go at lunch, and they'll pay you for it.

## Good habits from day one

- **Keep homes away from offices.** Condos and hotel rooms don't like sharing a floor with an office. Give them floors of their own. See [Tenant ratings](/help/tower/ratings/).
- **Watch the elevators at lunch.** If people wait too long, their floors start to fail. Add cars, or a second elevator, before it gets bad. See [Elevators](/help/building/elevators/).
- **Open the room card.** Pick **Inspect** and click any room to see how its tenants rate it and what's bothering them. They'll usually tell you exactly what's wrong.
- **Keep some money back.** Bills arrive whether or not rent does. See [Money](/help/tower/money/).
