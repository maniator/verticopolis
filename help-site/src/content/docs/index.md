Build a thriving high-rise and earn your way to a coveted **TOWER** rating.

Mouse: drag to pan with the Inspect tool; with a build tool, hold **Shift** (or Space) and drag to pan, and a middle-button drag pans too. Scroll to zoom, click to build, right-click or the Inspect tool to edit a room. Made a mistake? **Undo with Ctrl+Z** (or the Undo button). Redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z. Music changes with whatever part of the tower you're viewing. Try scrolling around!

Touch: press and drag on the tower to build (a ghost shows where the room lands), two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom, tap a room with Inspect to edit it. Full guide under **Playing on a phone**.
