---
title: Installing and updates
description: Install Verticopolis to your home screen or desktop to play offline and fullscreen, and how updates arrive without losing your tower.
---

Verticopolis runs in your web browser, and there's nothing to download. If you'd like it to feel more like an app, you can install it.

## Installing

Tap **Install** on the opening screen (or choose **File > Install app** in the game). Once installed, Verticopolis opens from your home screen or desktop in its own window, fullscreen, with no browser bar, and it plays offline.

- **On an iPhone or iPad,** Safari has no install button of its own. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up), then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **In other browsers,** look for **Install** or **Add to Home screen**, usually in the browser's main menu or at the end of the address bar. Not every browser offers it. If you don't see it, play for a moment and look again.

**Full screen without installing.** Choose **Options > Full Screen** to fill the screen with the game, and choose it again (or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>) to leave. Safari on an iPhone can't do this from a web page, so there the game tells you to add it to your home screen instead. Once the game is installed on a phone, it already fills the screen between the phone's own status bar and buttons, so the **Options** menu leaves **Full Screen** out there.

An installed game keeps its saves in the same place as the browser version on that device, so your towers are there either way.

## Playing offline

Once you've played online once, the whole game is stored on your device, the engine included, so it starts and runs with no connection at all. The help pages and the release notes need a connection.

## Updates

When a new version is ready, the game tells you with **Update available** and a summary of what's new. **Update now** saves your tower and reloads onto the new version, so you don't lose any progress. **Later** keeps you on the version you're playing, and the update applies the next time you open the game.

Every update's changes are in the [release notes](/help/changelog/).

The first time you open version 4 or any later release, a short fireworks show welcomes you, with links to [Your first tower](/help/first-tower/), [Classic and Modern](/help/classic-vs-modern/) and the [release notes](/help/changelog/), and a note for returning players about the new menu bar. The fireworks stop on their own; the welcome window stays until you close it with **OK**, its **✕**, the Escape key, or a click on the sky around it. Once you close it, it never shows again on that device and browser. If you reload or leave before closing it, it greets you again next time. With [reduced motion](/help/your-towers/settings/) on, the fireworks hold still.
