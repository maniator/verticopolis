title: Settings
description: The Verticopolis settings for sound, building, reduced motion and the steady clock, and what each one does.
---

**Settings…** is on the **Options** menu. Every setting applies right away. Sound and motion are remembered on this device for every tower; the building setting belongs to the tower you're playing.

## Sound

Three sliders set the levels of **Music**, **Ambience** (the sounds of the city and the tower) and **Effects** (building, the bulldozer and the rest). The sound button at the top of the screen, or **Options > Sound**, mutes everything at once.

The music follows what you're looking at, so it changes as you scroll up and down the tower.

The game goes quiet while you're somewhere else: in another tab, or in another program while the game's window is in the background. The sound comes back when you return, at the levels you set. If you muted it, it stays muted.

## Building <span class="vc-mode">Modern</span>

**Bridge floors between rooms.** With this on, when you drop a room or a run of floor near what you've already built, the game fills in the floor between them so people can walk across. With it off, separate sections of a floor stay apart: floors still appear under each room, they just never cross the gap. A new Modern tower starts with it off; tick it in the New Tower window or turn it on here at any time. Each Modern tower keeps its own choice, so a tower saved before keeps the one it had. A Classic tower always bridges, as in 1994, so it has no Building section. See [Floors and lobbies](/help/building/floors-and-lobbies/).

## Motion and pace

**Reduced motion.** Calms the background animation: weather, birds and the like. If your device is set to reduce motion, this stays on. It also holds the one-time welcome fireworks still (see [Installing and updates](/help/your-towers/installing/)).

**Steady clock.** As in 1994, the game's clock runs slowly through the lunch rush and quickly overnight, with a whole day taking the same real time. Turn this on for an even pace all day. See [Calendar and clock](/help/tower/clock/).

**Show decorative passers-by** <span class="vc-mode">Modern</span>. A few people stroll the corridors of busy floors to make the tower feel lived in. They aren't real tenants or visitors and don't affect the game. It starts off, so you see only the people on real trips; turn it on for a busier look. Reduced motion hides them even when it's on. See [People and traffic](/help/tower/people/).

## Screen saver

**Screen Saver…** is on the **Options** menu too. Like the screen savers of the 1990s, it fills the screen with something to watch while you're away. Pick one from the list and the little monitor plays it; **Preview** plays it full screen and brings you back to the dialog.

- **Night Shift** (the starting choice). Your own tower at night, windows switching on and off and a car gliding in each elevator shaft. A tower with no rooms yet gets a made-up one.
- **Skyline Starfield.** Flying through a night city whose stars are lit windows.
- **Express Elevators.** Elevator cars, riders aboard, drifting across the sky.
- **Endless Tower.** A crane builds made-up towers floor by floor, all night.
- **Bulletin Marquee.** Your tower's name, rating, population and funds scroll across the screen.

**Wait** sets how many minutes the tower must sit **paused** and untouched before the saver comes up. A running tower never starts it, so you can watch your tower as long as you like. **Also when I come back to this tab after that long** shows it when you return to the game's tab after being away at least that long; it starts on for a computer and off for a phone or tablet. Pick **(None)** to turn the screen saver off.

The tower stays paused while the saver is up and goes back to its speed after. Any key, click or tap, or moving the mouse, brings your tower back, and that press never builds or clicks anything. With reduced motion on, the saver shows one still picture. Your choices are remembered on this device.

## Appearance

**Appearance…** is on the **Options** menu, and on a computer the Control Panel has an **Appearance** icon too. It picks how the game's windows, menus and buttons look. Click a picture and press **OK**; **Cancel** leaves things as they were.

- **Standard** (the starting choice). Gray windows with navy title bars, as in 1994.
- **Skyline.** Softer windows with rounded tops, violet title bars and a dusk-sky desktop, a few years further along.
- **High Contrast.** White text and lines on black, with yellow title bars and a yellow highlight on the tool you've picked.

The scheme changes only the frame around your tower. The tower itself, its rules and its saves stay exactly the same, in Classic and Modern alike. Your choice is remembered on this device.

This manual wears the same scheme when you open it from the game, and keeps wearing it when you come back to it later on the same device, even after you change the scheme in the game. A small cookie that holds only the scheme's name carries it across (see [Privacy](/help/privacy/)).
