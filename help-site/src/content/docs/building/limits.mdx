---
title: Build limits
description: Every cap on what one Verticopolis tower can hold, from the 24 elevator shafts and 64 stair flights to the Metro and the Wedding Hall.
---


Most rooms you can build as many of as you can afford and find room for. A few are capped per tower, the same caps the 1994 game had. When you hit one, the game tells you which limit stopped the build.

## The lot

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Width | 375 tiles |
| Highest floor | 100 |
| Lowest floor | B9, nine floors underground |
| Sky lobbies | Floors 15, 30, 45, 60, 75 and 90 |

## Transport

| Limit | Covers | Cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elevator shafts | Standard, service and express together | 24 |
| Cars per shaft | Any elevator | 8 |
| Stairs and escalators | All flights together | 64 |
| Standard and service span | Floors one shaft may cover | 30 |
| Express span | Floors one shaft may cover | The whole tower |

The 24 shafts are one pool: an express doesn't get its own allowance. That is how the original worked, and it makes every express shaft a real decision.

## Rooms

| Room | Cap per tower |
| --- | --- |
| Security | 10 |
| Medical Center | 10 |
| Cinema | 16 |
| Party Hall | 16 |
| Aquatic Center <span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> | 8 |
| Metro Station | 1 (<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> 3) |
| Wedding Hall | 1 |

<span class="vc-mode">Classic</span> Cinemas and party halls share their 16 between them, as the 1994 save file kept one list for both.

<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> Cinemas and party halls each get 16 of their own.

<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> A tower may run up to three metro stations, all on one line beside the first ([Metro Station](/help/rooms/services/#metro-station)).

## What's unlocked

Most rooms also wait for a star rating before they appear in the build menu. Each room's page says which, and the [table of every room](/help/rooms/) lists them all.
