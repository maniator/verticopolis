Everybody builds something in the wrong place sooner or later. Verticopolis gives you two ways out: the bulldozer, and undo.

## Undo and redo

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> (or choose **Edit > Undo**, or press the **Undo** button) to take back your last change, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> (**Edit > Redo**) to put it back again. The game remembers your last **50** changes. Undo puts back the money too: undoing a room returns what you paid, and undoing a sale takes back what the sale refunded.

Undo covers what you build, sell and change. It doesn't turn back the clock: the money a room earned while it stood, the tenants who moved in or out, and events like fires stay as they happened. When a step can't be undone, the game tells you why.

## The bulldozer

Choose **Bulldoze** and click a room, a run of floor or a shaft to remove it. Selling a room returns about **half its price**; each room's fact box under [Rooms](/help/rooms/) shows exactly what it sells back for. Taking cars off an elevator returns half of each car's price too. Anyone riding a car you take off gives up the trip, and buying the car back (or undoing) doesn't put them on board again. People waiting for or walking to a shaft you remove find another way up, or walk back out if they had only just set out.

Drag with the bulldozer to clear a row in one sweep. A drag removes only the kind of thing it started on: start on a floor tile and it takes floor and leaves the elevators and rooms it crosses; start on an office and it takes offices. To remove something else, click it or start a new drag on it. A drag goes past anything the bulldozer can't take without a word, and when you let go it tells you why the last of them stayed. To clear everything a drag crosses, pick **Everything** under **Drag clears** on the strip that appears over the tower while the bulldozer is picked; **One kind** puts it back.

Some things to know:

- A room with a tenant loses its tenant. A sold [condominium](/help/rooms/homes/#condo) you bulldoze is gone along with its owners.
- A floor can't come out from under a room standing on it. Clear the room first.
- An elevator, stairway or escalator needs floor in its shaft on every floor it reaches, so the last tile under it stays until the shaft goes.
- A burned-out or bombed room stays a gutted shell until you bulldoze it and build again. See [Events](/help/tower/events/).
- While Security searches for a bomb, you can't build, bulldoze, sell, change an elevator, or undo and redo. Everything opens up again once the bomb is found or goes off. See [Events](/help/tower/events/#bomb-threats).

<span class="vc-mode">Classic</span> As in 1994, some rooms are for keeps once built: **Housekeeping, Security, Recycling Centers, cinemas and the Wedding Hall** can't be bulldozed. Neither can a lobby, on the ground or in the sky. And a floor comes out from either end inward: you can't take out a tile in the middle of a floor, because the floors above would be left with nothing to stand on.

<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> Every room can be sold, and you may open a gap in the middle of a floor. A lobby, on the ground or in the sky, can be bulldozed a tile at a time like floor, and a [tall one](/help/building/floors-and-lobbies/#tall-lobbies) returns half of what it cost on every story it rises, once nothing stands on the floor above the hall. That is how you part one ground lobby into two towers' front doors (see [Floors and lobbies](/help/building/floors-and-lobbies/#the-ground-lobby)). The last tile of ground lobby on the lot stays, so the lot always keeps a front door. A [sky bridge](/help/building/floors-and-lobbies/#sky-bridges) comes out whole: bulldoze any tile of it and the bridge comes down from tower to tower, once nobody is walking across. The sky lobby tile it hangs from stays until the bridge is gone, and one Undo puts the whole bridge back. Building one is one step too: a single Undo takes back the whole bridge the Sky Bridge tool laid.
