Everybody builds something in the wrong place sooner or later. Verticopolis gives you two ways out: the bulldozer, and undo.

## Undo and redo

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> (or the **Undo** button) to take back your last change, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> to put it back again. The game remembers your last **50** changes. Undo puts back the money too: undoing a room returns what you paid, and undoing a sale takes back what the sale refunded.

Undo covers what you build, sell and change. It doesn't turn back the clock: the money a room earned while it stood, the tenants who moved in or out, and events like fires stay as they happened. When a step can't be undone, the game tells you why.

## The bulldozer

Choose **Bulldoze** and click a room, a run of floor or a shaft to remove it. Selling a room returns about **half its price**; each room's fact box under [Rooms](/help/rooms/) shows exactly what it sells back for. Taking cars off an elevator returns half of each car's price too. Anyone riding a car you take off gives up the trip, and buying the car back (or undoing) doesn't put them on board again. People waiting for or walking to a shaft you remove find another way up, or walk back out if they had only just set out.

Drag with the bulldozer to clear a row in one sweep. A drag removes only the kind of thing it started on: start on a floor tile and it takes floor and leaves the elevators and rooms it crosses; start on an office and it takes offices. To remove something else, click it or start a new drag on it. To clear everything a drag crosses, pick **Everything** under **Drag clears** on the Bulldoze card (on a phone, in the strip over the tower); **One kind** puts it back.

Some things to know:

- A room with a tenant loses its tenant. A sold [condominium](/help/rooms/homes/#condo) you bulldoze is gone along with its owners.
- A floor can't come out from under a room standing on it. Clear the room first.
- An elevator, stairway or escalator needs floor in its shaft on every floor it reaches, so the last tile under it stays until the shaft goes.
- A burned-out or bombed room stays a gutted shell until you bulldoze it and build again. See [Events](/help/tower/events/).
- While Security searches for a bomb, you can't build, bulldoze, sell, change an elevator, or undo and redo. Everything opens up again once the bomb is found or goes off. See [Events](/help/tower/events/#bomb-threats).

<span class="vc-mode">Classic</span> As in 1994, some rooms are for keeps once built: **Housekeeping, Security, Recycling Centers, cinemas and the Wedding Hall** can't be bulldozed. Neither can a lobby, on the ground or in the sky. And a floor comes out from either end inward: you can't take out a tile in the middle of a floor, because the floors above would be left with nothing to stand on.

<span class="vc-mode">Modern</span> Every room can be sold, and you may open a gap in the middle of a floor. A sky lobby can be bulldozed a tile at a time like floor, and a [tall one](/help/building/floors-and-lobbies/#tall-lobbies) returns half of what it cost on every story it rises, once nothing stands on the floor above the hall. The ground lobby stays: it is the tower's front door.
