title: The basics
description: Floors, elevators, money and stars, the essentials of building a tower.
helpId: basics
---

- **Floors first.** Lay **Floor** tiles, then place rooms on them.
- **Move people.** Every floor needs an **elevator** or **stairs** chain back to the ground lobby, or tenants leave.
- **Make money.** Offices pay quarterly rent, condos sell once, hotels earn nightly, shops &amp; restaurants earn from foot traffic.
- **Grow your rating.** 2★ at 300 pop, 3★ at 1,000 (needs Security), 4★ at 5,000 (needs Medical, enough Recycling, suites &amp; a VIP), 5★ at 10,000 (needs a Metro). At 5★ a floor-100 payoff wins the game (under Going further).
- **Keep it moving.** People will ride as many elevators as it takes to reach a floor, but every transfer is another wait. Add **sky lobbies** (every ~15 floors) and **express** elevators so long trips stay quick; a tower that makes people transfer over and over crawls, and they give up.
