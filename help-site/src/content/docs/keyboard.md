---
title: Keyboard play
description: Every key for playing without a mouse.
helpId: keyboard
---

Play entirely without a mouse. Pick a tool in the palette (Tab to it, Enter to select), then:

- <kbd>↑</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd><kbd>←</kbd><kbd>→</kbd> (or <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd>) move the build cursor. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> for ×10
- <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> build (or inspect) at the cursor. For an elevator or stairway, press once to anchor and again at the far end to size the shaft
- <kbd>Delete</kbd> / <kbd>Backspace</kbd> / <kbd>X</kbd> bulldoze at the cursor · <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancel
- <kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>−</kbd> zoom · <kbd>C</kbd> re-center · <kbd>0</kbd>–<kbd>3</kbd> game speed · <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undo
- On the **Map** (Tab to it): <kbd>↑</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd><kbd>←</kbd><kbd>→</kbd> move the view (hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> for a whole screen) · <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> jump to the top or the bottom · <kbd>Esc</kbd> close
- On the **Map**'s **View** menu (Tab to it): <kbd>↑</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> pick the previous or the next view · <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> the first or the last
