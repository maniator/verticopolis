title: Going further
description: Recycling, winning, parking, theaters, venues, the Map, pricing, the clock and crash recovery.

- **Take out the trash.** One **Recycling Center** processes ~2,500 population. It visibly fills through the day and a garbage truck empties it each morning. Outgrow your centers and 4★ locks until you build more.
- **Win.** At 5★ with a Metro station, build the **Wedding Hall** (28 tiles wide and five floors tall) on floor 100 and pass the VIP inspection. The **TOWER** rank needs 15,000 occupants: office workers, residents, and venue customers. Hotel guests don't count at this stage, even when dining.
- **Parking** spaces only work when they touch a **Parking Ramp** or a connected space. Chain them off a ramp, or they sit empty. Offices want a space per ~24 workers (one per four offices) from 3★, and every hotel suite needs one of its own (the VIP drives).
- **Keep the films fresh.** A theater's audience falls as its film gets older. In Classic, inspect the theater and show a **New film** to fill it again, or a cheaper **Classic film** that never draws a full house. In Modern, pay once for a **Revival**, a **Feature** or a **Blockbuster**. A film draws for four weeks, fades for two, and then the theater books its **Auto-rebook** film again. A blockbuster costs the most but earns far more (great in a busy tower, a money-loser in a quiet one), and a revival is the cheap, safe pick for a small tower. A Modern theater has two screens, each with half the seats, its own film and its own Auto-rebook choice, and each pays for its own films: book them on different days so one screen always has a fresh film, or pair a blockbuster with a cheap revival. A Modern theater's **Ticket price** runs from Low to Premium: raise it while the theater sells out, and lower it when seats sit empty.
- **Place venues with care (Modern).** While you place a **Fitness Club**, **Spa**, **Daycare**, **Aquatic Center** or **Party Hall**, green bands mark the floors it helps, strongest nearest. A **Nightclub** shows violet bands for the floors its noise reaches at night. Shops, restaurants, other noisy venues and offices also bother rooms beside them on the same floor: while you place one, a violet band along its floor shows how far its noise carries, and while you place an office, condo, apartment or hotel room, an amber band shows the stretch that should stay clear of noisy neighbors. A lobby stops the noise. Inspect a condo, apartment or hotel room to see which amenity is helping it.
- **Find your way.** The **Map** window shows the whole tower, with a box around the part you're looking at. Click a spot on it to move the view there, or drag anywhere on it to slide the view. Close it with its ✕ and bring it back with the **Map** button.
- **Map views.** The **View** menu at the top of the Map changes what it shows. **Edit** is the plain tower. **Hotel** marks in red the hotel rooms that need cleaning or have cockroaches, with a count of each, and refreshes once per game hour. **Eval** colors each rated room Excellent, Good or Terrible by how its tenants or guests rate it, and **Pricing** colors each priced room High, Average, Low or Very Low by its rent. Both count their rooms, follow a price change at once and update their ratings once per game hour. **Traffic** colors each floor Clear, Busy or Jammed by how crowded its walkways and elevators are (a jammed floor is one where tenants start to leave), and **Occupancy** colors each floor with tenants or guests Mostly full, Half full or Mostly empty. Both count their floors and refresh once per game hour and after you build.
- **Price in bulk.** Inspect any office, condo or hotel room and use **“Set all …”** to re-price every unit of that kind at once (or reset them to the default). No need to edit each room. A preview shows how many change before you apply.
- **The clock breathes.** As in 1994, real time isn't spent evenly: the clock crawls through the lunch crush (watch your elevators earn their keep) and races through the small hours. A full day still takes the same real time, and the speed buttons still multiply it. Prefer an even pace? Toggle **Steady clock** in Settings.
- **If the game stops.** If the game engine crashes, the game reloads your tower from its last autosave. If it crashes again, the game stops and says so. Your saves stay on your device, and Reload tries again.
