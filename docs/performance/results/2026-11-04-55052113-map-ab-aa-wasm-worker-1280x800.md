# Overview Map open against closed (#1726), 2026-11-04

**Snapshot of 2026-11-04, not refreshing.** One run (2026-11-04 05:37Z to 05:55Z) of `npm run perf:worker -- --aa wasm --map-ab --scenarios large --viewport 1280x800 --reps 10 --out docs/performance/results/2026-11-04-55052113-map-ab-aa-wasm-worker-1280x800` (see [benchmark-protocol.md](../benchmark-protocol.md) and CAP-6 of `_bmad-output/specs/spec-overview-map/SPEC.md`) on main at `55052113` (app 2.81.0), which carries S5 (#1743, the Map shipped to players). Run it again for current figures; the arithmetic is pinned by `src/tests/perfWorker/mapAb.test.ts` and `analysis.test.ts`. The raw report, `results.json`, CSVs and run log are in [verticopolis-benchmarks](https://github.com/maniator/verticopolis-benchmarks/tree/main/results/2026-11-04-55052113-map-ab-aa-wasm-worker-1280x800) (private repository), not in this repo.

**These are statistics, not a verdict.** The protocol (section 6) passes a metric only when the upper bound of its paired interval stays within δ or ε, and no δ or ε is adopted yet (the 2026-11-03 calibration found M4 not adoptable, see [the calibration summary](2026-11-03-5cec61d-run-aa-wasm-worker-calibration.md)). So this run reports what was measured and what its intervals cannot rule out; it does not say the Map has no cost.

- Slot "ts" ran with the Map closed and slot "wasm" with it open, both on the WASM main-thread host of one build. The label is the slot's name, not the engine. Every ratio below is Map open over Map closed, taken from `ratios.csv`; each is the median of the 10 per-repetition paired ratios, so it need not equal the ratio of the two medians shown (for example 1x frame p99, 27.8 against 26.1 ms, prints x1.047).
- Windows PC, Ryzen 5 5600X, full Chromium 149.0.7827.55 (headless) on the RTX 3060 (ANGLE D3D11, hardware renderer), High performance power plan, clean tree, nothing else timed. 10 repetitions, order rotated, a fresh browser per repetition, 1x and 4x CPU throttle, large fixture (17,314 population), 1280x800 viewport, so the open Map is the desktop window. Validation passed with no errors.

## What it shows

- Medians and paired ratios for frame time, mean FPS, long tasks and command-to-visible-frame latency sit close to 1 (or 0 ms) at 1x and 4x, with no clamped frames and the sim at 120 minutes per second (118 to 119 at 4x) in both slots. The intervals are wide on several rows: 1x frame p99 (upper bound 1.854), 4x command to visible frame, p95 (1.84), 4x long tasks (+79 ms, above the 50 ms cap the M4 calibration applies). The FPS rows leave room too: the lower bounds are 0.993 (1x mean FPS), 0.948 (1x 1% low), 0.913 (4x mean FPS) and 0.919 (4x 1% low), so an FPS drop of up to about 9% at 4x and 5% at 1x is not ruled out. With 10 pairs and no adopted δ or ε, this run cannot rule out a regression of that size, and it does not claim to.
- Two costs whose intervals leave 1: the throttled (4x) cold start is about 4% slower with the Map open (x1.043, 1.021 to 1.081), and the 1x main-thread JS heap is about 0.6% larger (x1.006, 1.004 to 1.014; the combined heaps plus WASM memory row M6 reads is x1.005, 1.003 to 1.011, far under M6's ceiling of 1.25).
- Open against the 1x cold start there is no clear difference (x1.017, 0.925 to 1.081).
- Not shown: the phone card (a phone-sized viewport), the medium and small towers, a 4x memory reading, or the Map's own per-frame cost (`e2e/overviewMapPerf.spec.ts` measures that).
