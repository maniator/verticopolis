Verticopolis: a free SimTower clone you play in the browser

Verticopolis is a from-scratch, browser-based homage to the classic tower simulation SimTower (1994). Build a skyscraper floor by floor: place offices, condos, shops, restaurants, and hotels, wire them with elevators and escalators, set the rents, manage elevator traffic, and climb the star ratings from a single lobby all the way to a coveted 5-star tower. It is free, needs no download, and plays instantly in any modern browser. You can also install it as an app and play offline.
