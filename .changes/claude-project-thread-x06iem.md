## Player
- Decorative passers-by now walk only the corridors of occupied floors in Modern, at half the old count, and stay off lobbies, stairs, escalators and the space in front of elevators and shops.
- Classic towers no longer show decorative passers-by, matching the original.
- A new "Show decorative passers-by" setting turns them off in Modern, and reduced motion hides them.
- Decorative passers-by no longer turn red when the tower is stressed, so red always means a real person has waited too long.
## Engine
- The catalog's `rules` gains `decorativePassersBy` (catalog version 16): Classic false, Modern true.
