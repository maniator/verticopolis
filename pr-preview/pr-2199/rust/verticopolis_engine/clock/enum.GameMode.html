Enum GameMode 

Source 
pub enum GameMode {
    Classic,
    Modern,
}
Expand description

The two ways to play, chosen when a game starts.

Variants§

§

Classic

The 1994 original’s rules, prices and limits.

§

Modern

The original with quality-of-life changes and the calendar and bridging options. crate::catalog::catalog lists every difference a frontend shows.

pub fn cinema_screens(self) -> usize

How many screens a movie theater runs, each with its own film (#1950). SimTower’s theater shows one film; a Modern theater’s two floors are two screens with half the seats each.

pub fn halo_strength(self, halo: &Halo, d: f64, family_size: f64) -> f64

The hourly bonus or drain halo gives a room d floors from its venue whose household is family_size (read only by a family-scaled halo): the same values the hourly step applies, faded over the halo’s own floors from its own max.
