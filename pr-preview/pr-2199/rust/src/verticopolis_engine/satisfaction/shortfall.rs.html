1//! Service shortfalls that lower tenant evaluations: too little recycling for
2//! the tower's trash, and too little parking for its office workers.
3//!
4//! The original lowers evaluations when either runs short from three stars
5//! (the Classic reference: trash is an evaluation factor once recycling is
6//! needed, and offices want parking from three stars). It publishes no
7//! formula, so the shape here is ours, by mode (owner ruling, 2026-11-12):
8//!
9//! - **Classic** is the strict game. Any recycling shortfall sinks every
10//!   rated tenant to red, and tenants give notice as they do for any other
11//!   red rating, until the service is fixed. Offices sink the same way when
12//!   no parking is available to them: no working space is left once the
13//!   occupied hotel suites take theirs. The references name no
14//!   workers-per-space ratio (the wiki's Office article says offices lose
15//!   evaluation "if no parking is available"), and real five-star towers
16//!   saved by the original (TOWER6 and TOWER7 in
17//!   `conformance/fixtures/tdt-reddit`) park far fewer workers than our
18//!   ratio asks for, so Classic counts none. Distance to parking, which the
19//!   wiki's Evaluation article also names, waits on a PC check (#1524).
20//! - **Modern** is graduated. A small shortfall holds the affected rooms at
21//!   Good (they cannot read Excellent, and nobody leaves); a large one sinks
22//!   them: recycling more than one center short, or parking covering under
23//!   [`SHORTFALL_SINK_COVERAGE`] of what offices and homes need. Every
24//!   operational suite reserves its space first, and the rest go to the
25//!   tenanted offices and homes nearest the basement (lowest floor, then
26//!   leftmost): an office's six workers at
27//!   [`crate::services::PARKING_WORKERS_PER_SPACE`] workers a space, a home
28//!   its household share (#1973, `services::parking`), both halved while a
29//!   Metro station works. Only the rooms left without parking feel the
30//!   shortfall. A home short of parking is held at Good and never sinks, so
31//!   parking alone never empties one. The one-center allowance gives a tower
32//!   that just reached three stars room to afford its first center before
33//!   anyone leaves.
34//!
35//! Each mode's choices are named rules on `GameMode` in `rules/`.
36//!
37//! Recycling is tower-wide: every rated tenant shares the trash. An empty
38//! office the move-in gate weighs gets parking only if it would be parked.
39

49/// The star rating from which both services are demanded.
50pub const SHORTFALL_MIN_STAR: i64 = 3;
51/// Modern: parking coverage under which office ratings sink instead of
52/// holding.
53pub const SHORTFALL_SINK_COVERAGE: f64 = 0.5;
54/// Modern: recycling short by more than this much capacity (one center's
55/// worth) sinks ratings instead of holding them.
56pub const TRASH_SINK_MISSING_CAPACITY: f64 = RECYCLING_POP_PER_CENTER;
57/// The highest satisfaction a short-served room keeps: the Good line.
58pub const SHORTFALL_CAP: f64 = GRIPE_WARN;
59/// Hourly erosion of a sinking shortfall, the same pull unmet shop demand
60/// has. It outpaces a served room's hourly recovery even at the
61/// lowest rent, so a discount cannot hold a room at Good through it.
62pub const SHORTFALL_EROSION: f64 = 0.12;
63
64/// The gripe and vacate cause for too little recycling.
65pub const TRASH_CAUSE: &str = "trash";
66/// The gripe and vacate cause for too little office parking.
67pub const PARKING_CAUSE: &str = "parking";
68/// The gripe cause for a Modern home without parking. It holds the home at
69/// Good and never empties it.
70pub const HOME_PARKING_CAUSE: &str = "homeParking";

72/// The tower's service need and capacity this hour, read once per pass.
73#[derive(Clone, Debug, Default, PartialEq)]
74pub struct ServiceShortfalls {
75    /// The tower's mode once both services are demanded (from
76    /// [`SHORTFALL_MIN_STAR`]); `None` below it, when nothing falls short.
77    demanded_in: Option<GameMode>,
78    population: f64,
79    recycling_capacity: f64,
80    /// What the parking left after the hotel suites must serve. Classic:
81    /// the office workers. Modern: the spaces offices and homes need together.
82    parking_need: f64,
83    /// What that parking serves, in the same unit. Classic counts no ratio:
84    /// any free space serves every office (infinite capacity), and none
85    /// serves none. Modern: the spare spaces.
86    parking_capacity: f64,
87    /// Modern: the share of office and home demand the tower carries
88    /// (`Simulation::parking_demand_share`), for a newcomer's need.
89    parking_share: f64,
90    /// The first tenanted office (or, in Modern, home), in parking order
91    /// (floor, then x, then id), the spaces ran out before: it and every
92    /// room after it go unparked. `None` while every tenanted room parks.
93    first_unparked: Option<RoomOrder>,
94    /// A newcomer the move-in gate is weighing: its kind and population,
95    /// counted in the need while it is set.
96    candidate: Option<(Kind, f64)>,
97}
98
99/// `capacity` over `need` while it falls short; `None` once it covers the
100/// need (or nothing is needed).
101fn short_coverage(capacity: f64, need: f64) -> Option<f64> {
102    (need > 0.0 && need > capacity).then(|| capacity / need)
103}

105/// One service falling short for a room: its cause key, the share of the
106/// need the service meets tower-wide, and whether the shortfall sinks the
107/// room's rating.
108#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, PartialEq)]
109pub struct ServiceShort {
110    pub cause: &'static str,
111    pub coverage: f64,
112    pub sinks: bool,
113}
114
115impl ServiceShort {
116    /// Whether this shortfall may send a tenant packing. A Modern home short
117    /// of parking never leaves for it (#1973).
118    pub fn can_empty_a_room(self) -> bool {
119        self.cause != HOME_PARKING_CAUSE
120    }
121}
122
123/// The drain a shortfall puts on a room: held at Good, eroding when it sinks.
124pub fn shortfall_drain(short: ServiceShort) -> Drain {
125    Drain {
126        cap: SHORTFALL_CAP,
127        erosion: if short.sinks { SHORTFALL_EROSION } else { 0.0 },
128    }
129}

131/// Whether a kind's evaluation suffers from a trash shortfall: the rated
132/// tenants (offices, condos, hotel rooms and rentals).
133fn feels_trash(kind: Kind) -> bool {
134    kind == Kind::Office || kind == Kind::Condo || kind.is_hotel() || kind.is_rental()
135}
136
137/// A room's place in the parking order: floor, then x, then id.
138type RoomOrder = (i64, i64, i64);
139
140fn room_order(u: &Unit) -> RoomOrder {
141    (u.floor, u.x, u.id)
142}

144/// The workers one office brings, and so the parking it takes.
145fn office_workers_each() -> f64 {
146    Kind::Office.facility().population as f64
147}
148
149impl ServiceShortfalls {
150    fn candidate_population(&self) -> f64 {
151        self.candidate.map_or(0.0, |(_, pop)| pop)
152    }
153
154    /// The parking a newcomer adds to the need, in [`Self::parking_need`]'s
155    /// unit: an office's workers in Classic; in Modern its spaces, an
156    /// office's or one household's share.
157    fn candidate_parking_need(&self) -> f64 {
158        let Some((kind, pop)) = self.candidate else {
159            return 0.0;
160        };
161        match self.demanded_in.map(GameMode::office_parking) {
162            Some(OfficeParking::AnyFreeSpace) if kind == Kind::Office => pop,
163            Some(OfficeParking::WorkersPerSpace(_)) => {
164                modern_spaces_for(kind).unwrap_or(0.0) * self.parking_share
165            }
166            _ => 0.0,
167        }
168    }
169
170    /// The recycling shortfall, while there is one: every rated tenant
171    /// shares it.
172    pub fn trash(&self) -> Option<ServiceShort> {
173        let mode = self.demanded_in?;
174        let population = self.population + self.candidate_population();
175        let coverage = short_coverage(self.recycling_capacity, population)?;
176        let missing = population - self.recycling_capacity;
177        Some(ServiceShort {
178            cause: TRASH_CAUSE,
179            coverage,
180            sinks: mode.trash_shortfall_sinks(missing),
181        })
182    }
183
184    /// The share of the parking need the spaces left after the suites serve
185    /// tower-wide, while it falls short: the office workers in Classic, and
186    /// what offices and homes need together in Modern.
187    pub fn parking_coverage(&self) -> Option<f64> {
188        self.demanded_in?;
189        let need = self.parking_need + self.candidate_parking_need();
190        short_coverage(self.parking_capacity + SHARE_EPSILON, need)
191            .map(|_| (self.parking_capacity / need).min(1.0))
192    }
193
194    /// The parking shortfall room `u` feels: none when it holds a space (a
195    /// tenanted room the allocation reached), or, for a room the gate is
196    /// weighing, when the spaces cover the need with it counted. Offices feel
197    /// it in both modes; homes only in Modern, where it holds them at Good and
198    /// never sinks them.
199    pub fn parking_for(&self, u: &Unit) -> Option<ServiceShort> {
200        let mode = self.demanded_in?;
201        let cause = match u.kind {
202            Kind::Office => PARKING_CAUSE,
203            kind if mode.homes_need_parking() && is_parked_home(kind) => HOME_PARKING_CAUSE,
204            _ => return None,
205        };
206        let coverage = self.parking_coverage()?;
207        let unparked = if u.is_tenanted() {
208            self.first_unparked
209                .is_some_and(|first| room_order(u) >= first)
210        } else {
211            true
212        };
213        unparked.then_some(ServiceShort {
214            cause,
215            coverage,
216            sinks: cause == PARKING_CAUSE && mode.office_parking_shortfall_sinks(coverage),
217        })
218    }
219
220    /// Counts a newcomer of `kind` and `population` in the need (the move-in
221    /// gate's probe), or stops counting one with `None`.
222    pub fn set_candidate(&mut self, candidate: Option<(Kind, f64)>) {
223        self.candidate = candidate;
224    }
225
226    /// The shortfall that binds room `u`: a sinking one before one that only
227    /// holds the rating, then the worse-covered of the two. A room off the
228    /// network already has the worse problem, so `served` must be true.
229    pub fn binding_for(&self, u: &Unit, served: bool) -> Option<ServiceShort> {
230        if !served {
231            return None;
232        }
233        let trash = self.trash().filter(|_| feels_trash(u.kind));
234        let parking = self.parking_for(u);
235        match (trash, parking) {
236            (Some(t), Some(p)) => {
237                let parking_binds = (p.sinks, -p.coverage) > (t.sinks, -t.coverage);
238                Some(if parking_binds { p } else { t })
239            }
240            (t, p) => t.or(p),
241        }
242    }
243
244    /// The tower-wide coverage behind `cause`, for the inspector's line.
245    pub fn coverage_of(&self, cause: &str) -> Option<f64> {
246        match cause {
247            TRASH_CAUSE => self.trash().map(|t| t.coverage),
248            PARKING_CAUSE | HOME_PARKING_CAUSE => self.parking_coverage(),
249            _ => None,
250        }
251    }
252}

254#[doc(hidden)]
255impl Simulation {
256    /// This hour's service shortfalls. Below [`SHORTFALL_MIN_STAR`] neither
257    /// service is demanded, so the scans are skipped.
258    pub fn service_shortfalls(&self) -> ServiceShortfalls {
259        if self.star < SHORTFALL_MIN_STAR {
260            return ServiceShortfalls::default();
261        }
262        let d = self.parking_demand();
263        let spots = self.tower.functional_parking_spots() as f64;
264        let share = self.parking_demand_share();
265        let (parking_need, parking_capacity) = match self.mode.office_parking() {
266            OfficeParking::AnyFreeSpace => {
267                let guests_parked = self
268                    .tower
269                    .units_of_kind(Kind::HotelSuite)
270                    .filter(|u| u.is_present())
271                    .count() as f64;
272                let capacity = if spots > guests_parked {
273                    f64::INFINITY
274                } else {
275                    0.0
276                };
277                (d.office_pop, capacity)
278            }
279            OfficeParking::WorkersPerSpace(_) => {
280                (d.shared_need, self.parking_spare_after_suites(&d))
281            }
282        };
283        let first_unparked = if parking_need > parking_capacity + SHARE_EPSILON {
284            match self.mode.office_parking() {
285                OfficeParking::AnyFreeSpace => self.first_unparked_office(parking_capacity),
286                // Offices alone all take the same share: select the boundary.
287                OfficeParking::WorkersPerSpace(workers) if d.home_need == 0.0 => {
288                    self.first_unparked_office(parking_capacity * workers / share)
289                }
290                OfficeParking::WorkersPerSpace(_) => {
291                    self.first_unparked_room(parking_capacity, share)
292                }
293            }
294        } else {
295            None
296        };
297        ServiceShortfalls {
298            demanded_in: Some(self.mode),
299            population: self.tower.total_population() as f64,
300            recycling_capacity: recycling_capacity_of(self.recycling_centers()),
301            parking_need,
302            parking_capacity,
303            parking_share: share,
304            first_unparked,
305            candidate: None,
306        }
307    }
308
309    /// The first tenanted office the spaces run out before, with `capacity`
310    /// office workers served. Only the boundary matters, so a linear
311    /// selection does.
312    fn first_unparked_office(&self, capacity: f64) -> Option<RoomOrder> {
313        let mut offices: Vec<RoomOrder> = self
314            .tower
315            .units_of_kind(Kind::Office)
316            .filter(|u| u.is_tenanted())
317            .map(room_order)
318            .collect();
319        let parked = (capacity / office_workers_each()).floor() as usize;
320        (parked < offices.len()).then(|| *offices.select_nth_unstable(parked).1)
321    }
322
323    /// Modern: the first tenanted office or home, in parking order, whose
324    /// share would overrun the `spare` spaces. Shares differ by kind, so the
325    /// boundary is found in two linear passes instead of a sort of every
326    /// room: one sums each floor's shares and finds the floor the spaces run
327    /// out on, and only that floor's rooms are put in order and walked.
328    fn first_unparked_room(&self, spare: f64, share: f64) -> Option<RoomOrder> {
329        let shares = || {
330            self.tower
331                .room_units()
332                .filter(|u| u.is_tenanted())
333                .filter_map(move |u| Some((u, modern_spaces_for(u.kind)? * share)))
334        };
335        let mut per_floor = [0.0_f64; (MAX_FLOOR - MIN_FLOOR + 1) as usize];
336        for (u, spaces) in shares() {
337            per_floor[(u.floor - MIN_FLOOR) as usize] += spaces;
338        }
339        let limit = spare + SHARE_EPSILON;
340        let mut used = 0.0;
341        let mut boundary = None;
342        for (i, &spaces) in per_floor.iter().enumerate() {
343            if used + spaces > limit {
344                boundary = Some(i as i64 + MIN_FLOOR);
345                break;
346            }
347            used += spaces;
348        }
349        let floor = boundary?;
350        let mut rooms: Vec<(RoomOrder, f64)> = shares()
351            .filter(|(u, _)| u.floor == floor)
352            .map(|(u, spaces)| (room_order(u), spaces))
353            .collect();
354        rooms.sort_unstable_by_key(|&(order, _)| order);
355        let last = rooms.last().map(|&(order, _)| order);
356        // The floor's sum overran the spaces, so one of its rooms does; the
357        // last stands in should rounding in a different order say otherwise.
358        rooms
359            .into_iter()
360            .find_map(|(order, spaces)| {
361                used += spaces;
362                (used > limit).then_some(order)
363            })
364            .or(last)
365    }
