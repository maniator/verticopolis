1//! Whether a step meets the tower it expects (`undo.rs`), checked before it
2//! changes anything. The steps apply one after another, so the tower a step
3//! meets is the one its edit left, moved on only by time; a step that finds
4//! anything else (an edit made outside every gesture) is refused, so its cash
5//! never moves for an edit that is not there to reverse, nothing it puts back
6//! lands on a room or a shaft that took its place, hangs where its floor was
7//! taken away, breaks a rule of the lobbies or the metro station, or goes past
8//! a cap its build kept to (`sim_undo_ground.rs`, `sim_undo_caps.rs`). Nor does
9//! a floor or lobby tile it takes out leave a room, a shaft or the story above
10//! hanging, as a sale never does (`Tower::removal_reason`), or a ground-floor
11//! lobby tile cut a run of ground-floor or basement tiles laid in part after it
12//! off from the oldest ground-floor tile of the part it stood in (in play the
13//! tower's first lobby tile, unless a sale parted the ground floor), though one
14//! such run with a lobby tile may stand on its own, as a tower's first lobby
15//! tile does, where no older ground-floor tile of that part stays or comes back
16//! with the step outside it (`Ground::lobbies_leave_nothing_hanging` says which
17//! tiles count).
18//!
19//! Time moves a build on without any edit, and the owner's rule for that is
20//! that a step takes a build back at its full price only while nobody has
21//! used it since the step was made. A room the edit built is in use once it
22//! opens; one that was already open is in use once a tenant moved in, it
23//! earned or it burned ([`UnitUse`]). A floor or lobby tile, or a shaft, is
24//! in use once an hourly pass has run with it standing (`Step::hour_passes`),
25//! and a shaft also once anyone rode it (`Transport::rides`), which also
26//! keeps an undo from taking away the cars or floors an edit added to it. A
27//! used build is sold instead, at the sale price. A build whose dig searched
28//! for treasure is never undone: the dig and its luck stay, so the search
29//! would cost nothing.

39/// Why a step was not applied. Nothing changed. `sellable` says the player
40/// can sell the build instead, which a lobby tile the mode keeps and a
41/// Classic permanent room no fire gutted never allow, and a burning room
42/// allows only once the fire is out.
43#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, PartialEq, Eq)]
44pub(crate) enum StepRefused {
45    /// The step needs more cash than the tower has.
46    NotEnoughMoney,
47    /// A room the step would take out at its full price has been used
48    /// since the step was made.
49    InUse { sellable: bool },
50    /// A floor or lobby tile the step would take out at its full price has
51    /// stood through an hourly pass since the step was made, which moves
52    /// tenants in over the floors the tower reaches. Floor a room's build
53    /// laid under it is held to this too, while the room is still being
54    /// built; once the room opens, the room's own reason is the one given,
55    /// though a fire, which waits, gives way to this one. `sellable` says
56    /// the player could sell everything the step takes out instead: it is
57    /// false for a lobby tile, a burning room or a Classic room that stays
58    /// for good, a tile the step lays again, and while anything but a room
59    /// or shaft the step takes out stands on one of the step's tiles, a
60    /// shaft would be left with no floor on a story it spans once they are
61    /// sold, or something the step takes out is gone since.
62    GroundUsed { sellable: bool },
63    /// A room the step would take out was being built when the step was made
64    /// and has opened since.
65    Opened { sellable: bool },
66    /// A shaft the step would take out, or take cars or floors from, at the
67    /// price paid has been used since the step was made.
68    ShaftUsed,
69    /// A build the edit took out again inside its own gesture had been used
70    /// by then (`Step::used_in_gesture`).
71    UsedThenSold,
72    /// The edit dug new ground that could hold treasure. `sellable` is as
73    /// for `GroundUsed`, and false when the step takes nothing out: a build
74    /// its own gesture sold again leaves nothing to sell.
75    SearchedForTreasure { sellable: bool },
76    /// A room the step would take out is burning.
77    OnFire,
78    /// Security is searching for a bomb, and the tower takes no edits until
79    /// the search ends ([`edit_refusal`](crate::sim::Simulation::edit_refusal)).
80    BombSearch,
81    /// The step would reprice a condo that has been sold since: a sold
82    /// condo's price is fixed, as repricing it is refused.
83    PriceFixed,
84    /// A sale the step would make again meets a room in another use than
85    /// the one it was sold in: a tenant moved in or out since.
86    UseChanged,
87    /// The tower around the step has changed: a unit or shaft the step would
88    /// put back has no space, no floor under it, a lobby, a room or the metro
89    /// station where the build rules refuse it, or no place under its cap, or a
90    /// floor or lobby tile it would take out holds up a room, a shaft or the
91    /// story above, or taking out a ground-floor lobby tile, with the tiles
92    /// there it takes with it, cuts a run of ground-floor or basement tiles
93    /// laid in part after it off from the oldest ground-floor tile of the part
94    /// it stood in (in play the tower's first lobby tile; one run with a lobby
95    /// tile may stand on its own where no older ground-floor tile of that part
96    /// stays or comes back with the step outside it). Only an edit clears it
97    /// (time alone takes no unit out), and undoing that edit puts the block
98    /// back, so the step never applies.
99    Blocked,
100    /// The tower is not the one the step expects: something it would take
101    /// out or restore is gone or has changed, or something it would put back
102    /// is there already.
103    Stale,
104}

106impl StepRefused {
107    /// Whether a step refused for this is dropped, as one that will not
108    /// apply again. Only time can make a step apply, and time does not run
109    /// back: a build in use stays in use, a sold condo seldom empties, a
110    /// room's tenants and income seldom go back to just what a sale found,
111    /// and a tower an edit changed under the step changes back only by
112    /// another edit, whose own step sits above this one. Time does bring in
113    /// money, put a fire out and end a bomb search.
114    pub(crate) fn is_lasting(self) -> bool {
115        !matches!(
116            self,
117            StepRefused::NotEnoughMoney | StepRefused::OnFire | StepRefused::BombSearch
118        )
119    }
120}

122/// What a check found of the floor and lobby tiles a step would take out.
123#[derive(Default)]
124struct Tiles {
125    /// One the step built has stood through an hourly pass.
126    used: bool,
127    /// One holds up something the step leaves.
128    blocked: bool,
129}
130
131/// Whether the player could sell instead everything a step would take out:
132/// a room that is not burning, of a kind that can be sold, and a floor tile
133/// once the rooms on it are sold, when it holds up nothing else. Only a
134/// refusal that gives advice reads it, so a check that can give none asks
135/// nothing (`asked`).
136struct Sale {
137    asked: bool,
138    takes_any: bool,
139    sellable: bool,
140}
141
142impl Sale {
143    fn new(asked: bool) -> Sale {
144        Sale {
145            asked,
146            takes_any: false,
147            sellable: true,
148        }
149    }
150
151    /// Note a build the step takes out, which the player could sell first
152    /// when `sells` says so. It is asked only while the rest could be.
153    fn note(&mut self, sells: impl FnOnce() -> bool) {
154        if self.asked {
155            self.takes_any = true;
156            self.sellable = self.sellable && sells();
157        }
158    }
159
160    /// Whether the step takes anything out and the player could sell it
161    /// all: a build its own gesture sold again leaves nothing to sell.
162    fn works(&self) -> bool {
163        self.takes_any && self.sellable
164    }
165}

167/// The refusals a check has found. A lasting one is reported before any
168/// other, so a refusal that time can clear never keeps a step that can
169/// never apply.
170#[derive(Default)]
171struct Found {
172    lasting: Option<StepRefused>,
173    passing: Option<StepRefused>,
174}
175
176impl Found {
177    fn add(&mut self, why: StepRefused) {
178        let slot = if why.is_lasting() {
179            &mut self.lasting
180        } else {
181            &mut self.passing
182        };
183        slot.get_or_insert(why);
184    }
185
186    /// Whether a lasting reason is found, which only the dig's displaces.
187    fn has_lasting(&self) -> bool {
188        self.lasting.is_some()
189    }
190
191    /// Report lasting reason `why` before anything found so far.
192    fn put_first(&mut self, why: StepRefused) {
193        debug_assert!(why.is_lasting());
194        self.lasting = Some(why);
195    }
196
197    fn verdict(self) -> Result<(), StepRefused> {
198        match self.lasting.or(self.passing) {
199            Some(why) => Err(why),
200            None => Ok(()),
201        }
202    }
203}

206impl Simulation {
207    /// Refuse `step` unless the tower is the one it expects and the balance
208    /// can pay for it.
209    pub(crate) fn check_undo_step(&self, step: &Step) -> Result<(), StepRefused> {
210        let mut found = Found::default();
211        let leaving_units: FastSet<i64> = step
212            .units
213            .iter()
214            .filter(|img| matches!(img.change, UnitChange::TakeOut(_)))
215            .map(|img| img.id)
216            .collect();
217        let ground = Ground::of(&self.tower, step, &leaving_units);
218        let hour_passed = self.on_hour_runs != step.hour_passes;
219        // The tiles' reasons come after the rooms', which say more.
220        let mut tiles = Tiles::default();
221        // Of the refusals, only a floor's use and a dig give advice for the
222        // whole step.
223        let mut sale = Sale::new(hour_passed || step.searched_for_treasure);
224        for img in &step.units {
225            match (&img.change, self.tower.get_unit(img.id)) {
226                (UnitChange::TakeOut(held), Some(now)) if now.kind.is_structural() => {
227                    let hangs = !ground.leaves_nothing_hanging_from(now);
228                    sale.note(|| {
229                        !hangs
230                            && !ground.lays_again(now)
231                            && self.sells(now)
232                            && ground.sells_from_an_end(now)
233                    });
234                    if now.state == UnitState::Fire {
235                        found.add(StepRefused::OnFire);
236                    } else if !img.built {
237                        self.check_take_out(&mut found, false, held, now);
238                    } else {
239                        tiles.used |= hour_passed;
240                    }
241                    tiles.blocked |= hangs;
242                }
243                (UnitChange::TakeOut(held), Some(now)) => {
244                    sale.note(|| self.sells(now));
245                    self.check_take_out(&mut found, img.built, held, now);
246                }
247                (UnitChange::PutBack(want), None) => {
248                    if !self
249                        .tower
250                        .tiles_free_for(want, |id| leaving_units.contains(&id))
251                        || !ground.under_room(want)
252                    {
253                        found.add(StepRefused::Blocked);
254                    }
255                }
256                (UnitChange::Restore { want, left }, Some(now)) => {
257                    if reprices_a_sold_condo(now, want, left) {
258                        found.add(StepRefused::PriceFixed);
259                    }
260                }
261                (UnitChange::TakeOut(_), None) => {
262                    sale.note(|| false);
263                    found.add(StepRefused::Stale);
264                }
265                _ => found.add(StepRefused::Stale),
266            }
267        }
268        for img in &step.transports {
269            if matches!(img.change, TransportChange::TakeOut { .. }) {
270                // A shaft can be sold while it stands.
271                sale.note(|| self.tower.get_transport(img.id).is_some());
272            }
273        }
274        let named: FastSet<i64> = step.transports.iter().map(|img| img.id).collect();
275        // Selling the step's tiles from under a shaft would leave it over
276        // nothing, so the advice holds only while each shaft the step does
277        // not take out keeps its floor as it stands now. A sale lays no
278        // tile, so the tiles the step lays hold up no shaft here. Only a
279        // floor's use and a dig that takes something out read it, and only
280        // while the rest could be sold.
281        let advised = (tiles.used && sale.sellable) || (step.searched_for_treasure && sale.works());
282        let shafts_keep_floor = advised
283            && self
284                .tower
285                .transports
286                .iter()
287                .filter(|t| !named.contains(&t.id))
288                .all(|t| ground.still_under_shaft_once_sold(t))
289            && self.restored_shafts_stand(step, &ground);
290        if tiles.used {
291            found.add(StepRefused::GroundUsed {
292                sellable: sale.sellable && shafts_keep_floor,
293            });
294        }
295        self.check_shafts(&mut found, step, &named, &ground, hour_passed);
296        // What the step's tiles hold up, and its caps, come after the
297        // builds' own reasons. Each is lasting, so it is asked only while it
298        // could be the reason given: no lasting reason is found yet, and no
299        // dig will be put first.
300        if !found.has_lasting() && !step.searched_for_treasure {
301            // A shaft the step leaves alone keeps its floor.
302            let shafts_stand = self
303                .tower
304                .transports
305                .iter()
306                .filter(|t| !named.contains(&t.id))
307                .all(|t| ground.still_under_shaft(t));
308            if tiles.blocked
309                || !shafts_stand
310                || !ground.lobbies_leave_nothing_hanging()
311                || !within_caps(&self.tower, step)
312            {
313                found.add(StepRefused::Blocked);
314            }
315        }
316        // After the builds' own reasons, which say more.
317        if step.used_in_gesture {
318            found.add(StepRefused::UsedThenSold);
319        }
320        if step.money < 0.0 && self.money + step.money < 0.0 {
321            found.add(StepRefused::NotEnoughMoney);
322        }
323        // The dig and its luck stay whatever else the check found.
324        if step.searched_for_treasure {
325            found.put_first(StepRefused::SearchedForTreasure {
326                sellable: sale.works() && shafts_keep_floor,
327            });
328        }
329        found.verdict()
330    }

332    /// Check a unit `now` that a step would take out, which the step found
333    /// in use `held`; `built` as [`crate::undo::UnitImage`] has it. A floor
334    /// or lobby tile the step built is checked by the hour instead.
335    fn check_take_out(&self, found: &mut Found, built: bool, held: &UnitUse, now: &Unit) {
336        if now.state == UnitState::Fire {
337            // Its use shows once the fire is out.
338            found.add(StepRefused::OnFire);
339            return;
340        }
341        let sellable = self.sells(now);
342        let use_now = UnitUse::of(now);
343        if !built {
344            // A sale made again prices the room by its use as sold.
345            if use_now.as_sold() != held.as_sold() {
346                found.add(StepRefused::UseChanged);
347            }
348        } else if held.only_opened(use_now) {
349            found.add(StepRefused::Opened { sellable });
350        } else if use_now != *held {
351            found.add(StepRefused::InUse { sellable });
352        }
353    }

355    /// Check the shafts a step names, and the shafts it leaves standing on
356    /// the floor and lobby tiles it takes out.
357    fn check_shafts(
358        &self,
359        found: &mut Found,
360        step: &Step,
361        named: &FastSet<i64>,
362        ground: &Ground,
363        hour_passed: bool,
364    ) {
365        // `named` holds the shafts the step names. Each one's image is a
366        // shaft as it stood beside the others when the step was made, so
367        // they never clash with each other: one shrunk by the step makes
368        // room for another it puts back.
369        // A shaft the step puts back or gives its old span needs floor in
370        // its column on each story of that span and no other shaft there,
371        // as building it did. Classic's rule that keeps an escalator off an
372        // office floor is not asked: an office may go in beside one, so a
373        // tower with both is one that building could make.
374        let fits = |want: &Transport| {
375            ground.under_shaft(want)
376                && self
377                    .tower
378                    .transports
379                    .iter()
380                    .filter(|t| !named.contains(&t.id))
381                    .all(|t| !t.clashes_with(want))
382        };
383        for img in &step.transports {
384            let now = self.tower.get_transport(img.id);
385            let (as_left, room, takes_back) = match (&img.change, now) {
386                (TransportChange::TakeOut { left }, Some(now)) => (now.config_eq(left), true, true),
387                (TransportChange::Restore { want, left }, Some(now)) => (
388                    now.config_eq(left),
389                    fits(want),
390                    takes_cars_or_floors(left, want),
391                ),
392                (TransportChange::PutBack(want), None) => (true, fits(want), false),
393                _ => (false, true, false),
394            };
395            if !as_left {
396                found.add(StepRefused::Stale);
397                continue;
398            }
399            if !room {
400                found.add(StepRefused::Blocked);
401            }
402            if img.built && takes_back && (hour_passed || ridden_since(now, &img.change)) {
403                found.add(StepRefused::ShaftUsed);
404            }
405        }
406    }

408    /// Whether the player could sell unit `u` instead of undoing its build,
409    /// as `Simulation::remove_facility` lets them: a lobby tile the mode
410    /// keeps stays for good (`Tower::lobby_stays`), a burning unit waits for
411    /// the fire, and a Classic permanent room stays unless a fire gutted it.
412    fn sells(&self, u: &Unit) -> bool {
413        !self.tower.lobby_stays(u)
414            && u.state != UnitState::Fire
415            && !self.stays_for_good(u.kind, u.state)
416    }
417
418    /// Whether each shaft `step` gives another span or other cars still
419    /// has floor where the step takes it from, as the shaft stands now and
420    /// a sale of what the step takes out would leave it.
421    fn restored_shafts_stand(&self, step: &Step, ground: &Ground) -> bool {
422        step.transports
423            .iter()
424            .filter(|img| matches!(img.change, TransportChange::Restore { .. }))
425            .filter_map(|img| self.tower.get_transport(img.id))
426            .all(|t| ground.still_under_shaft_once_sold(t))
427    }
428}

430/// Whether putting `want`'s span and cars on a shaft that is `left` takes
431/// cars or floors away from it.
432pub(crate) fn takes_cars_or_floors(left: &Transport, want: &Transport) -> bool {
433    want.cars < left.cars || want.bottom > left.bottom || want.top < left.top
434}

436/// Whether the shaft `now` has been ridden since the step that expects it
437/// as `change` leaves it was made.
438fn ridden_since(now: Option<&Transport>, change: &TransportChange) -> bool {
439    let left = match change {
440        TransportChange::TakeOut { left } | TransportChange::Restore { left, .. } => left,
441        TransportChange::PutBack(_) => return false,
442    };
443    now.is_some_and(|now| now.rides != left.rides)
444}

446/// Whether restoring `want` (where the edit left `left`) onto `now` would
447/// change the price of a condo that has sold, which `price_unit` and
448/// `set_no_rate` (`rent.rs`) refuse.
449fn reprices_a_sold_condo(now: &Unit, want: &UnitFacts, left: &UnitFacts) -> bool {
450    now.kind == Kind::Condo
451        && now.ever_occupied
452        && (puts_back(&want.rent, &left.rent, &now.rent)
453            || puts_back(&want.no_rate, &left.no_rate, &now.no_rate))
